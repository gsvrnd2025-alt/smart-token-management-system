WiFi Configuration Instructions
  1. Boot Setup Portal: Hold GPIO 4 button and press reset. LED will flash fast.
  2. Connect to AP: Join the open WiFi Smart-Token-Dispenser on your phone.
  3. Open Portal: Page loads automatically, or navigate to http://192.168.4.1.
  4. Save WiFi: Select your SSID, enter password, and click Connect. ESP32 will reboot.
Done!
